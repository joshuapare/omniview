SAFETY AND OWNERSHIP
Direct create/edit are qualified by discovery, create/update verbs, admission and ownership. Ordinary spec is mutable; pinned CRD has no extra immutable spec rules. Kubernetes identity immutable; system metadata/status not form-owned. GitOps-managed fixture offers review/inspection but no save or ownership takeover. Denied/missing/unavailable/empty/unsupported are distinct fixture selections; production lookups must retain unresolved references and draft. Source/field edits do not perform requests. Review includes destination and impact, never successful creation. No implicit providers/workloads/controllers installed.
